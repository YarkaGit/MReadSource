<h1 align="center">MRead - Minimalist Ebook Reader</h1>

<p align="center">
  MRead is a fast, lightweight, and local ebook reader for those who appreciate comfort and minimalist design. Zero ads, no paid subscriptions, and no data tracking.
</p>

### 🌟 Key Features:
* **Supported formats:** EPUB, FB2, FB2.ZIP, TXT.
* **Smart Online Tools:** Highlight text for instant translation or look up definitions via integrated web-dictionaries.
* **Complete Customization:** Light, dark, and cream themes, custom fonts, margins, and line spacing.
* **Private & Local Library:** Your books are stored locally on your device. No backend servers, no cloud syncs - your reading privacy is guaranteed.
