# Privacy Policy for MRead

Last updated: 2026-06-19

MRead is a local, offline ebook reader. Your privacy is the default.

## What we collect
Nothing. MRead does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We have no servers.

## Where your data lives
Your books, reading progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes, and settings are stored only locally on your device. Backups you create are saved to a folder you choose on your own device.

## Internet use
MRead works offline. The internet is used only when you actively request it:
- Translation and dictionary lookups. When you select a word or passage and ask for a translation or definition, that selected text is sent to third-party services (Wiktionary and Free Dictionary API) to return a result. Nothing else is sent, and the request is not linked to your identity.

If you never use translation or dictionary lookups, MRead makes no network requests.

## Permissions
- Internet: used only for the translation and dictionary lookups described above.
- All files access (storage): used only to let you open book files from any folder on your device and to create local backups. Your files are never uploaded anywhere.

## Children
MRead is a general-purpose reader and is not directed at children.

## Your rights
Since MRead does not collect or store any personal data on our side, there is no personal data to access, correct, or delete. Removing the app removes all local app data from your device.
